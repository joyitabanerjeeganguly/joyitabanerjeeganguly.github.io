I'm Joyita Banerjee Ganguly

• PhD Candidate • Researcher • Instructor

I am a final-year Mathematics Ph.D. student (expected graduation: May 2027) at Central Michigan University, working under the supervision of Professor Debraj Chakrabarti.
In May 2026, I have earned my second Master's degree (Master's of Arts in Mathematics) from Central Michigan University.
I earned my Bachelor's degree from Bethune College affiliated by Calcutta University, India, and my first Master's degree (Master's of Science) in Applied Mathematics from the University of Hyderabad, India.
My research focuses on complex analysis and differential geometry, particularly the geometry of Hermitian quadrics in complex projective space.
My dissertation studies the volume of domains bounded by Hermitian quadrics with respect to the Fubini–Study metric. I obtained an explicit rational formula for these volumes in terms of the positive and negative eigenvalues of the defining Hermitian matrix. The formula naturally leads to Schur polynomials and connections with symmetric function theory.
As part of this project, I am developing an independent proof of the Dual Cauchy Identity, which plays a central role in the symmetric-function formulation of the volume formula. I am also studying the geometry of complex projective space and the Fubini–Study metric to further understand the geometric ideas underlying my work./H5>
