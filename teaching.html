Teaching Philosophy

I believe that every student can learn mathematics when the classroom is welcoming, expectations are high, and support is available. I begin by learning my students' names and building an atmosphere where they feel comfortable asking questions. I introduce formal ideas through pictures, concrete examples, and technology before moving to abstraction, and I use active learning so students practice making decisions and explaining their reasoning. My goal is to help students see mathematics as something they can do, while providing the structure and encouragement they need to meet high expectations.
